About Us

Letnyaya Zolotitsa

Impressions that stay with you.

Letnyaya Zolotitsa is a lodge on the White Sea coast, among the pine forests of Onega Pomorye National Park.

Forbes included Letnyaya Zolotitsa among Russia’s ten best country hotels. Following reconstruction, the lodge begins a new chapter with two buildings and 24 rooms.

Time by the White Sea.

Find out more about our hotel

Discover Letnyaya Zolotitsa

Rooms

Twenty-four rooms across two buildings: 19 in the Sea building and five with a separate entrance in the Taiga building.

Cuisine

Pomor and Russian cuisine, fish and seafood, northern berries and mushrooms.

Care and service

Our team will help you plan your stay.

Experiences

Explore northern nature and the culture of the White Sea coast.

Activities

Discover seasonal routes and activities in the surrounding landscape.

White Sea spa

A pool, hammam, rasul, Pomor sauna and four treatment rooms, as well as a Russian bath and outdoor tubs.
